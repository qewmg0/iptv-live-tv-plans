## Is a 24-hour IPTV trial worth using?

For this kind of service, a short trial can answer questions that a pricing table cannot.

During a trial, it makes sense to check the channels you actually watch instead of randomly browsing the catalogue. Try live sports if that is your main reason for subscribing. Test evening streaming when your home connection is busy. Open the service on the device you intend to use most often.

TiviLive currently says its free trial provides access to the full advertised service for 24 hours, without requiring a credit card.

There is also a practical reason not to judge an IPTV live TV subscription purely by the advertised number of channels. “28,000 channels” sounds enormous, but what matters to a viewer is whether the specific channels they care about are available, stable and appropriate for their region.

## What should you check before paying?

The first thing is the exact content you want. If you mainly watch US sports, international news or particular entertainment networks, check those categories specifically rather than assuming a large channel count guarantees everything you want.

Next, check the connection limit. A one-connection plan may be perfectly adequate for a single viewer, but a household trying to watch different streams simultaneously needs a multi-connection option. TiviLive currently lists plans from one through five simultaneous connections.

Finally, look beyond the headline price. The provider advertises a 7-day money-back guarantee, but independent customer feedback is mixed. Trustpilot currently shows **2.9/5 from five reviews**, with complaints involving service interruptions, support and refunds alongside some positive comments. That is a very small review sample, so it should be treated as a signal rather than a definitive measure of service quality.

## One more thing about IPTV legality

IPTV itself is simply a method of delivering television over an internet connection. The important question is whether a particular provider has the necessary rights to distribute the channels, movies and sports content it offers.

That matters here because TiviLive's public pages emphasize very large channel and VOD numbers, but the material reviewed does not independently establish licensing rights for the advertised catalogue. Independent coverage also notes that the headline channel figures are provider-supplied rather than independently audited.

For anyone comparing an IPTV live TV subscription, that is worth checking before treating the price alone as the deciding factor.

TiviLive currently advertises a **24-hour free trial**, paid plans starting at **$15 for one month**, longer subscriptions up to **12 months for $100 with one connection**, and higher-priced options for households that need multiple simultaneous connections.
